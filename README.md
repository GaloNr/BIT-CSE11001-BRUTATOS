## Project's Purpose

Our materials teach a complete beginner how a computer works inside, how computers talk to each other, and how an AI can act intelligently by reacting to the world instead of reasoning about it. The three parts are linked by one running example: **Dusty, a small robot vacuum**, which needs hardware to sense and move, a network to receive commands and updates, and behaviorist AI to decide what to do.

## Intended Learners

First-year university students from any major who use computers and phones every day but have never studied computer science. We assume they can read basic English, do school-level arithmetic, and know what a file, an app and a website are. We do not assume any programming, electronics or networking knowledge.

The full list of learning goals, the order of topics, authors, reviewers and the status of every section are in [MODULEOUTLINE.md](MODULEOUTLINE.md).
